Owner sign in

Use the Firebase owner account with the private admin permission. This is separate from your Apps Script dashboard passcode.

After clicking the link in your email, refresh this page and sign in again. Email verification does not itself grant owner access.

Customer lookup

Only look up customers who have opted into Sweet Club. Confirm their email against their completed bakery order.

Completed order punch

Give exactly one punch after you verify the order is completed and eligible. The order reference must match the order in your bakery spreadsheet or email.

Retrying the same order ID won't award another punch. Never use the customer's email as an order ID.

Transfer paper punches

For a customer bringing an existing physical punch card. Check the paper card and mark those punches transferred so they cannot be reused.

Redeem free Signature Pack

Only redeem an issued reward after Tina accepts the customer's reward request. This does not automatically create a bakery order or reserve pickup time.

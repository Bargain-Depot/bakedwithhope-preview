THE HEART BEHIND THE BAKES

More than a mini cake.

Baking has been something I always enjoyed doing just for fun, but through personal experiences like my dad being deported and everything happening around us in the world today, I started thinking about how I could turn something I enjoy into something with purpose.

That's when I decided I wanted my business, Baked with Hope, to be more than just desserts. My goal through this business is to raise money through selling my desserts to one day be able to afford for my family and I to reunite with my dad in Vietnam, while also giving back to immigrant families through donations to organizations like RAICES.

Thank you so much for all your love and support, it truly means the world to me.

- Tina

Order Policy & Info 🤍

PLACING YOUR ORDER

  • Orders must be placed through my website link in bio.
  • For bulk orders, please send me a message.
  • Once your order is submitted, you should receive a confirmation email.

PICKUP 🚗

  • The night before pickup you will receive a reminder text as well as the address for pickup.
  • Pickup is by appointment only during the time/date selected when ordering.
  • If you’re running late to your pickup, please send me a message!

PAYMENTS $

  • Payment is due within 24 hours after placing your order.
  • If paying with cash, a 50% deposit through Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle is required. That deposit will go toward your total, and the remainder will be paid in cash at pickup.
  • If payment/deposit isn’t sent within 24 hours, your order will be cancelled.

CANCELLATIONS 🚫

  • If you need to cancel your order or change your pickup date, please let me know 3–4 days in advance.
  • Please note that if you choose to cancel your order less than 24 hours before your scheduled pickup time, your deposit/payment will not be refunded.

ALLERGY INFORMATION 🍰

  • If you have any allergies, please let me know before placing your order and I’ll be happy to answer any questions!
